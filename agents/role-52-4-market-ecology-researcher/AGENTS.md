## Role description supplied by owner

52.4 Market Ecology Researcher
Staffing applicability: Conditional: strategies materially exposed to crowding, endogenous liquidity, participant behaviour or rapid competitive adaptation.
Reports to: Quantitative Research Director; works with Market Microstructure Researcher (5.12), Crowding Analyst (38.3) and trading teams.
Role purpose: Research how heterogeneous market participants, structural market design and interactions among strategies affect alpha persistence and liquidity.
Principal responsibilities:
- Study public or lawfully licensed proxies for participant classes, flows, order-book behaviour, dealer inventory and ETF/index rebalancing, stating limits on unobservable trader identities.
- Model feedback among liquidity provision, trend following, hedging and de-risking to identify hypotheses about transient dislocations, correlated exits and structural decay.
- Evaluate changes in tick rules, fee structures, execution technology and product adoption for effects on market ecology.
- Build falsifiable agent-based or reduced-form models and compare findings with empirically observed market outcomes, allowing for alternative explanations.
- Analyse prospective strategy interaction and likely crowding under scaling without assuming a fixed AUM threshold determines when the role becomes necessary.
Required deliverables: Market-ecology research reports; participant-proxy assumptions ledger; scenario and competition models; falsifiable alpha-decay hypotheses.
Performance indicators: Reproducibility; out-of-sample explanatory value; calibration to observed liquidity and price effects; usefulness of risk and capacity referrals.
Qualifications / expertise: Market microstructure, empirical asset pricing, statistical modelling, simulation and exchange-market structure.
Decision rights / interfaces: Research and scenario recommendations only; cannot claim access to confidential rival positions or direct trading decisions. CRO independently assesses any risk implications.
Integration / overlap: Distinct from the Crowding Analyst's direct measurement of crowding and the Market Microstructure Researcher's order-level studies; focuses on system-level participant interactions and strategic adaptation.
