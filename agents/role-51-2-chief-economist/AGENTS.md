## Role description supplied by owner

51.2 Chief Economist
Staffing applicability: Conditional: global macro, multi-asset and interest-rate-sensitive mandates requiring a central economic research view.
Reports to: CIO; provides scenario analyses to CRO and Investment Committee; may direct the Macro Research Analyst and coordinate with the Macro Regime Forecaster.
Role purpose: Lead firm-wide macroeconomic analysis, establish consistent economic assumptions and communicate uncertainty across rates, inflation, growth, currencies and geopolitical scenarios.
Principal responsibilities:
- Own the economic research calendar, macro indicator definitions, forecast vintages and an explicit baseline/upside/downside scenario framework, preserving dissenting research views.
- Coordinate inflation, labour-market, productivity, fiscal-policy, monetary-policy, rates and currency analysis across geographic and investment teams.
- Develop coherent cross-asset transmission scenarios and distinguish observed releases, external consensus, analyst interpretation and genuinely out-of-sample forecasts.
- Maintain a dated macro assumption library covering publication lags, central-bank calendars, yield curves, currency regimes and data revisions.
- Coordinate geopolitical event research with the relevant specialist, distinguish factual developments from contested claims and avoid expressing party or electoral preferences on behalf of the firm.
- Brief the CIO, macro portfolio managers and independent risk teams on forecast uncertainty, structural breaks, tail scenarios and adverse outcomes.
- Evaluate economic forecast quality through documented hindsight reviews and comparison with simple or market-implied benchmarks.
Required deliverables: Monthly and event-driven global macro outlook; forecast vintage database; rates/inflation/FX scenario deck; cross-asset scenario transmission map; forecast-error review.
Performance indicators: Forecast calibration and revision timeliness; source accuracy; scenario coherence; documented uncertainty; decision-usefulness assessed independently of whether markets happened to move in the forecast direction.
Qualifications / expertise: Advanced macroeconomics, monetary and international economics, econometrics, cross-asset market experience and leadership of economic research.
Decision rights / interfaces: Owns the firm's coordinated research baseline, not portfolio trading decisions, regulatory policy or independent risk limits. A PM may document an alternative scenario within an approved investment mandate; the CRO independently determines risk treatment.
Integration / overlap: Provides leadership to existing Macro Research Analyst (6.3) and coordinates with Macro Portfolio Manager (4.7), Regime Forecasting Specialist (38.5) and Macro Regime Forecaster (50.9), rather than replacing their respective research/portfolio roles.

52. QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH — PORTFOLIO OF IDEAS, ALPHA PIPELINE AND MARKET NETWORKS
Reporting and oversight: Quantitative Research Director / CIO; all prospective live strategies require independent validation, approved investment mandates and separate capital allocation approval.
